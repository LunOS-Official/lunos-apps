# Lun Store - Content Policy, Uploader Terms, Takedown

## Uploader declaration (checkbox + timestamped record on every upload)

> I declare that this content is **my own work**, or that I hold the
> **permission/license** to publish it here. Any copyright, trademark or
> legal claim arising from this content is **solely my (the uploader's)
> responsibility**, not that of Lun Store/LunOS. A false declaration can
> lead to account termination.

Shown on the upload page in plain words: **"Upload your own work. Do not
upload other people's paid themes, game assets, fonts, music or code
without permission. If in doubt, do not upload it."**

## Prohibited

- Copyrighted/trademarked content without permission
- Malware, phishing, fraud, real-money gambling
- NSFW content, hate speech, incitement to violence
- Impersonation of another person or project

## License

Every upload must carry a license (MIT, Apache-2.0, GPL, CC-BY, CC0, or
"Freeware - redistribution allowed, no sale"). Content without a license
is not listed.

## Report and Takedown (notice-and-action)

1. Every package page has a **Report** button; copyright contact (Grievance):
   `abuse@<domain>`.
2. On a complaint, the content is **hidden within 24-72 hours** pending review.
3. Valid complaint -> removal + strike for the uploader. Invalid complaint ->
   uploader counter-notice, content restored.
4. 3 reports (from different users) -> auto-hide pending review.
5. 3 strikes -> publisher banned.

## Platform position

Lun Store is a user-content marketplace; every item is the uploader's
responsibility. We run scanning and review in good faith but do not
guarantee third-party content. The installer always verifies SHA-256
and shows permissions before installing.

## Privacy (summary)

Account email/name (via Google sign-in), comments, ratings, download
counts, favorites/sync - nothing more. Data is never sold. Deleting an
account deletes personal data. User files are never synced.

_Fyn (virtual currency) has no cash value; it cannot be purchased with or
exchanged for real money. Developer real-money premium features run
through their own external channels - the store only shows the link._

_This document is platform design, not legal advice._
